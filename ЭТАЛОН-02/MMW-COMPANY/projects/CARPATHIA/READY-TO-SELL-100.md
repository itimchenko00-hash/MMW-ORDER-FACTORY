# CARPATHIA — READY-TO-SELL BUSINESS PROJECT

Developed by MMW-COMPANY. Implementation is performed by the buyer/operator/partner.

## 01 PROJECT IDENTITY
Carpathian hospitality and experience platform combining lodging, wellness, local food, routes, B2B retreats, events, transfers and partnerships.

## 02 BUSINESS MODEL
Monetize accommodation, experiences and ancillary services while increasing utilization through B2C, B2B, events and partner channels.

## 03 MARKET
Guests, families, wellness customers, tourists, corporate groups, event clients and tourism partners. Local demand, seasonality, ADR, occupancy and competitive set require site-specific validation.

## 04 PRODUCT
Existing canonical catalog includes Eco Lodge, Family Lodge, Wellness Day, Wellness Retreat, Carpathian Breakfast, Local Food Box, Mountain Route, Private Carpathians, Corporate Retreat, Private Event, Airport/City Transfer and CARPATHIA Partner.

## 05 OPERATING MODEL
Property/site → accommodation → wellness/food/routes → booking → guest service → transfers/events → partner network → repeat demand.

## 06 FINANCIAL MODEL
Current public catalog prices are UAH base prices; currency switch is informational. Existing catalog examples include 4,200 UAH/night Eco Lodge, 6,500 UAH/night Family Lodge, 1,500 UAH/person Wellness Day, 8,900 UAH/package Wellness Retreat and other service tariffs. These are product prices, not a full P&L. Full occupancy, ADR, OPEX, CAPEX and seasonality model must be validated.

## 07 LEGAL & COMPLIANCE
Site/land rights, accommodation classification and local rules, construction/renovation approvals, food safety, fire safety, labor, environmental/waste, transport and event requirements must be checked for the actual operating format.

## 08 IMPLEMENTATION PACKAGE
Location/site → concept → property/fit-out → permits → suppliers → staffing → booking/sales channels → soft launch → full operation → partner expansion.

## 09 SALES / INVESTMENT PACKAGE
Sell as a complete hospitality business concept with catalog, operating model, site criteria, financial model, launch plan, sales channels, partner model and acquisition/implementation terms.

## 10 DATA ROOM
Site criteria, concept, accommodation standards, service catalog, pricing matrix, seasonality model, CAPEX/OPEX, staffing, supplier list, legal checklist, booking/sales plan, marketing assets, operating SOPs, risks and buyer handover checklist.
