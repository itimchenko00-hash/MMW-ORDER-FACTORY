# AGROHUB — READY-TO-SELL BUSINESS PROJECT

Developed by MMW-COMPANY. Implementation is performed by the buyer/investor/operator.

## 01 PROJECT IDENTITY
Agro infrastructure and processing platform: field → receiving → storage → processing → packaging → sales.

## 02 BUSINESS MODEL
Create value by processing agricultural raw material and monetizing value uplift per tonne; additional economics can come from infrastructure utilization and partner services.

## 03 MARKET
Target customers: farmers/producers, food buyers, processors, logistics partners and investors. Site-specific demand, contracts and competitive pricing must be validated before investment.

## 04 PRODUCT
Core offer: receiving, storage, drying/cleaning, processing, packaging and market access. Existing website contains a Yield Engine demonstration.

## 05 OPERATING MODEL
Secure raw-material supply, receive and grade, store, process, package, sell and control throughput, yield, losses and quality.

## 06 FINANCIAL MODEL
Current demonstration inputs: 800 t/month; 72% yield; 576 t finished product; 14,500 UAH/t finished product; raw material 6,800 UAH/t; processing 1,800 UAH/t. Demonstration: sales 8.352M UAH; raw material 5.440M; processing 1.440M; value uplift 1.472M UAH/month before other costs. This is not a full P&L or investment forecast.

## 07 LEGAL & COMPLIANCE
Required package must be site- and product-specific: land/use rights, construction and engineering approvals where applicable, food/product safety requirements, labor, environmental/waste rules, fire safety, metrology/quality documentation and contracts. Final legal checklist is required before launch.

## 08 IMPLEMENTATION PACKAGE
Demand validation → site → engineering → pilot line → commissioning → operating launch → regional scale.

## 09 SALES / INVESTMENT PACKAGE
Sell as a business-project package containing concept, operating flow, Yield Engine, CAPEX/OPEX model, market validation, supplier quotes, implementation roadmap and investor memorandum. Do not present demonstration economics as guaranteed profit.

## 10 DATA ROOM
Required handover set: concept, site brief, technical specification, equipment list, supplier quotations, process map, staffing plan, legal checklist, financial model, sensitivity analysis, risk register, launch plan, sales plan and buyer handover checklist.
