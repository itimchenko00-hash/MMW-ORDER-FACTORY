# AGROHUB — READY-TO-SELL PRODUCT PACK

**Product owner:** MMW-COMPANY  
**Status:** READY-TO-SELL BUSINESS PROJECT  
**Implementation:** buyer / investor / operating partner

## Product
Infrastructure platform connecting raw agricultural supply with receiving, storage, processing, packaging and sales. The value proposition is added margin per tonne rather than raw-material turnover alone.

## Buyer profile
Agro investor, regional operator, food processor, agricultural group, logistics partner or landowner seeking an integrated processing asset.

## Package delivered by MMW-COMPANY
- concept and positioning;
- value-chain architecture;
- operating model;
- Yield Engine financial demonstration;
- CAPEX/OPEX assumptions framework;
- site and infrastructure checklist;
- implementation roadmap;
- investor/commercial presentation;
- adaptation specification for a selected site.

## Implementation phases
1. Demand and feedstock validation.
2. Site and utilities due diligence.
3. Pilot processing configuration.
4. Offtake and sales contracts.
5. Financing and construction.
6. Commissioning and scale.

## Financial rule
Published calculator values are a demonstration, not a forecast. Buyer model must add logistics, energy, losses, personnel, taxes, working capital, CAPEX and financing.

## Commercial form
Sale / license / project-development agreement. Optional MMW-COMPANY advisory is separate.
