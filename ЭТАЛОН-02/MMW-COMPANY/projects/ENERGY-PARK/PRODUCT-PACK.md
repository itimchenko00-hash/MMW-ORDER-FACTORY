# ENERGY-PARK — READY-TO-SELL PRODUCT PACK

**Product owner:** MMW-COMPANY  
**Status:** READY-TO-SELL BUSINESS PROJECT  
**Implementation:** buyer / investor / infrastructure partner

## Product
Energy and industrial cluster combining generation, storage, grid connection and commercial/production demand into one infrastructure asset.

## Buyer profile
Energy investor, industrial developer, landowner, infrastructure fund, anchor consumer or regional development partner.

## Package delivered by MMW-COMPANY
- energy-infrastructure concept;
- generation / storage / demand architecture;
- Yield Map demonstration model;
- site and grid due-diligence framework;
- tenant / anchor-consumer model;
- CAPEX/OPEX assumptions structure;
- implementation roadmap;
- investor/commercial package;
- adaptation specification.

## Implementation phases
1. Load-profile and demand validation.
2. Site, grid and legal due diligence.
3. Generation/storage configuration.
4. Anchor tenants and contracts.
5. Financing, construction and connection.
6. Operations and replication.

## Financial rule
Yield Map is a demonstration. Final economics require actual tariffs, connection costs, CAPEX, degradation, storage, taxes, permits, financing and load profile.

## Commercial form
Project sale / license / development partnership. Optional MMW-COMPANY launch support is separate.
