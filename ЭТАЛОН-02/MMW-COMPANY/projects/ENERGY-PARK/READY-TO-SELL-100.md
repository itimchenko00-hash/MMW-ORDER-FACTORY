# ENERGY-PARK — READY-TO-SELL BUSINESS PROJECT

Developed by MMW-COMPANY. Implementation is performed by the buyer/investor/operator.

## 01 PROJECT IDENTITY
Energy-generation project concept built around an 800 kW solar/renewable generation asset with self-use and surplus realization.

## 02 BUSINESS MODEL
Create economic value through electricity generated for self-consumption and monetized surplus, subject to site, grid, tariff, regulation and contract conditions.

## 03 MARKET
Potential buyers/users include industrial or commercial electricity consumers and energy-market counterparties. Actual offtake, grid capacity, tariff and market conditions must be validated for the selected site.

## 04 PRODUCT
Core product is energy generation capacity plus electricity value from self-use and surplus sales/realization.

## 05 OPERATING MODEL
Site → design → grid/connection → equipment procurement → construction/installation → commissioning → generation monitoring → self-use/offtake → maintenance.

## 06 FINANCIAL MODEL
Current demonstration inputs: 800 kW; 1,050 kWh/kW-year; 840,000 kWh/year; self-use 571,200 kWh; surplus 268,800 kWh; electricity price 7.2 UAH/kWh; surplus realization 45%. Demonstration gross annual value: self-use 4,112,640 UAH + surplus 870,912 UAH = 4,983,552 UAH (~4.98M). This is gross value, not EBITDA/net profit or guaranteed revenue.

## 07 LEGAL & COMPLIANCE
Land rights, planning, grid connection, construction/electrical approvals, metering, energy-market/offtake contracts, environmental and safety requirements, taxation and applicable Ukrainian energy rules must be validated for the actual project.

## 08 IMPLEMENTATION PACKAGE
Site/load study → resource assessment → grid feasibility → design → CAPEX quotes → financing → permits/connection → procurement → construction → commissioning → operation.

## 09 SALES / INVESTMENT PACKAGE
Investment package must show generation assumptions, self-use/offtake structure, CAPEX, OPEX, degradation, downtime, tariffs, sensitivity, financing and payback. Current 4.98M figure is a model output, not an investment promise.

## 10 DATA ROOM
Site/load data, solar/resource study, single-line/grid concept, equipment specification, supplier quotes, EPC scope, permits checklist, CAPEX/OPEX model, offtake assumptions, sensitivity model, risks, schedule, maintenance plan and handover checklist.
