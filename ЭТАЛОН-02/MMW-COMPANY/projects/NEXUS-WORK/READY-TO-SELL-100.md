# NEXUS WORK — READY-TO-SELL BUSINESS PROJECT

Developed by MMW-COMPANY. Implementation is performed by the buyer/operator/partner.

## 01 PROJECT IDENTITY
Flexible work / operating-space business concept monetizing usable square meters through occupancy and service uplift.

## 02 BUSINESS MODEL
Monthly revenue combines occupied space × price per m² with a service/experience uplift. The buyer implements the physical site and operating model.

## 03 MARKET
Target users can include independent professionals, teams, small businesses and corporate clients seeking flexible workspace/services. Local demand, competitive rates, occupancy and churn require validation.

## 04 PRODUCT
Workspace/service packages built around rentable or serviceable area, occupancy and added services. The existing presentation contains the core business logic and calculation basis.

## 05 OPERATING MODEL
Site/fit-out → sales channels → memberships/bookings → space utilization → customer service → ancillary services → retention → occupancy optimization.

## 06 FINANCIAL MODEL
Current demonstration: 2,500 m² × 650 UAH/m² × 72% occupancy × 1.12 service uplift ≈ 1.31M UAH/month gross revenue. OPEX/NOI are demonstration assumptions and require validation for rent/ownership, utilities, staffing, taxes, fit-out, churn and financing.

## 07 LEGAL & COMPLIANCE
Premises/lease rights, intended use, fire and occupational safety, accessibility, labor, taxation, utilities, signage and any sector-specific permissions must be validated for the actual site.

## 08 IMPLEMENTATION PACKAGE
Location → demand study → lease/ownership → fit-out → service design → staffing → presales → soft launch → occupancy ramp → operating optimization.

## 09 SALES / INVESTMENT PACKAGE
Buyer package: concept, customer segments, product/pricing structure, occupancy model, site requirements, CAPEX/OPEX, launch plan, sales channels, KPI model, risks and acquisition terms.

## 10 DATA ROOM
Site criteria, layout/space program, fit-out brief, equipment/furniture list, supplier quotes, staffing plan, pricing matrix, occupancy/churn assumptions, CAPEX/OPEX model, sales plan, SOPs, legal checklist, risks and handover checklist.
