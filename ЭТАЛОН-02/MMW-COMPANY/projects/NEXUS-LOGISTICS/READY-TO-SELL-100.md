# NEXUS LOGISTICS — READY-TO-SELL BUSINESS PROJECT

Developed by MMW-COMPANY. Implementation is performed by the buyer/operator/partner.

## 01 PROJECT IDENTITY
Logistics business project focused on structured transport operations, route utilization, fleet/partner capacity and service sales.

## 02 BUSINESS MODEL
Revenue is generated from paid transport routes/services. Unit economics are driven by route volume, price, utilization and variable route cost.

## 03 MARKET
Potential customers: businesses requiring recurring freight/transport services, shippers, distributors and partner logistics channels. Route density, demand, competition and contract rates require validation for the launch geography.

## 04 PRODUCT
Core offer: contracted logistics/transport routes and related services. Existing project page contains the project catalog and route economics.

## 05 OPERATING MODEL
Demand/contracts → route planning → vehicle/partner allocation → dispatch → transport → proof of delivery → invoicing → utilization and margin control.

## 06 FINANCIAL MODEL
Current demonstration: 90 routes × 28,000 UAH × 78% = 1,965,600 UAH revenue; variable route cost 90 × 420 km × 19 UAH/km = 718,200 UAH; contribution 1,247,400 UAH; contribution margin ≈63.4%. This is contribution economics, not EBITDA/net profit. Fixed costs, taxes, financing and other OPEX are separate.

## 07 LEGAL & COMPLIANCE
Transport licensing/permit requirements where applicable, vehicle and driver documentation, insurance, labor, cargo documentation, safety, tax and cross-border/customs requirements where applicable must be validated for the actual route model.

## 08 IMPLEMENTATION PACKAGE
Market/route validation → anchor contracts → fleet/partner capacity → dispatch system → pilot routes → KPI control → scale route network.

## 09 SALES / INVESTMENT PACKAGE
Buyer package: service catalog, route model, target customer profile, sales pipeline structure, unit economics, fleet/partner model, operating SOPs, financial model, launch plan and acquisition terms.

## 10 DATA ROOM
Route assumptions, tariffs, cost matrix, fleet requirements, supplier/partner contracts, licenses/checklists, insurance, staffing, dispatch SOPs, KPI dashboard specification, financial model, sales plan, risks and handover checklist.
