# ALADIN — READY-TO-SELL BUSINESS PROJECT

Developed by MMW-COMPANY. Implementation is performed by the buyer/investor/development partner.

## 01 PROJECT IDENTITY
ALADIN RESIDENCE: standardized development concept combining land analysis, product, architecture, engineering, economics, construction, sales, operation and scaling.

## 02 BUSINESS MODEL
Package a development concept into an investable/implementable project and monetize the development product through acquisition/implementation by a buyer or partner.

## 03 MARKET
Primary focus: residential/development demand, initially around Ivano-Frankivsk and comparable Ukrainian locations. Site-specific demand, absorption, price and competition must be validated for each land plot.

## 04 PRODUCT
Existing site defines TERRA, ARCHITECTURA and INGENIUM plus commercial packages: ALADIN Concept, Architectural Package, Townhouse Pre-Project, Development Package, Land Due Diligence, Technical Audit, Investor Package, 3D Visualization, Energy Concept, START, DEVELOPMENT and FULL CYCLE.

## 05 OPERATING MODEL
Land → due diligence → product concept → architecture → engineering/energy → feasibility → financing → construction → sales → handover/operation → scale.

## 06 FINANCIAL MODEL
Current demonstration base: 1,800 m² saleable area; 52,000 UAH/m²; 72% sales; construction 36,773 UAH/m², plus land, infrastructure, soft costs, marketing, finance and 7% reserve. The current base scenario is not proven profitable and must be treated as a feasibility signal, not a profit promise.

## 07 LEGAL & COMPLIANCE
Land title/use, zoning/planning constraints, construction documentation, utility connections, permits/notifications, design documentation, labor, fire and technical requirements, contracts, taxation and applicable Ukrainian construction-system requirements must be validated for the actual site.

## 08 IMPLEMENTATION PACKAGE
Site shortlist → due diligence → concept → feasibility → design → financing → approvals → construction → sales → commissioning/handover.

## 09 SALES / INVESTMENT PACKAGE
Buyer receives project concept, development logic, financial model, architectural/product package, implementation roadmap, investor materials and acquisition terms. All forecasts must be labelled assumptions until site and supplier data are confirmed.

## 10 DATA ROOM
Land brief, due-diligence checklist, master concept, architectural package, engineering concept, CAPEX/OPEX model, sensitivity scenarios, legal checklist, supplier/contractor quotes, schedule, sales model, investor memorandum, risks and handover checklist.

## COMPLETENESS GATE
Concept: PRESENT. Product architecture: PRESENT. Commercial catalog: PRESENT. Development flow: PRESENT. Demonstration finance: PRESENT. Site-specific feasibility, validated CAPEX, legal package and investment-grade economics: REQUIRED.
