# MMW-COMPANY commercial pricing provenance

Date of benchmark: 2026-10-06

## Principle

MMW-COMPANY prices are published as market-oriented starting estimates, not invented universal fixed tariffs. The public catalog states that final scope, timeline and price are confirmed after analysis of the client's task.

## MMW calculation method

The catalog converts market hourly benchmarks into understandable project starting estimates using an estimated effort envelope for a typical small/medium project. Prices are rounded and are not presented as guaranteed market tariffs.

Examples:
- 9,000 UAH ≈ 5 hours at a $40/hour benchmark.
- 27,000 UAH ≈ 15 hours.
- 45,000 UAH ≈ 25 hours.
- 65,000 UAH ≈ 36 hours.

For complex development, engineering, investment or management work, the public price is only an entry point and the final commercial offer is individual.

## Exclusions

Published estimates do not include external costs such as state fees, paid databases, surveys, geodesy, laboratory work, licensed specialists, contractor costs, construction, equipment, travel or other third-party expenses unless explicitly included in a confirmed proposal.
