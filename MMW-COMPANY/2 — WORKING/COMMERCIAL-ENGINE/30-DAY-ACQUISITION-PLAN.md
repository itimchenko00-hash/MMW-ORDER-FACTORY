# 30-DAY ACQUISITION PLAN

## Week 1 — Build the machine
- Finalize offer catalogue and proposal template.
- Create CRM with at least 50 qualified prospects.
- Prepare one-page project cards for six portfolio projects.
- Prepare 3 outreach scripts.
- Define one booking/contact route.

## Week 2 — Direct sales
- Add 10–15 qualified prospects per working day.
- Send personalized outreach daily.
- Run qualification calls.
- Sell BUSINESS AUDIT as the entry product.
- Record objections and refine the offer.

## Week 3 — Convert
- Deliver first audits quickly.
- Convert audits into Business Model / Financial Model / Investment Package work.
- Start targeted outreach for the six READY-TO-SELL projects.
- Approach landowners with ALADIN adaptation offer.

## Week 4 — Scale what works
- Double down on the best-performing channel.
- Publish one useful case/insight per week.
- Ask every satisfied client for one referral.
- Review pipeline and cash.
- Prepare first project-sale or partnership negotiation.

## Activity target
100 targeted prospects, 30 replies, 15 qualification calls, 5 paid audits, 2 larger projects.

## Cash target
The first objective is not vanity traffic. It is the first paid audit, then the first larger project, then the first READY-TO-SELL transaction.
