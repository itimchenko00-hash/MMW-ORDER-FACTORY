## What an acquisition should include
- Project identity and business model.
- Product/service catalog.
- Operating model.
- Financial assumptions and model status.
- Technical and legal contour.
- Implementation roadmap.
- Sales/investment package.
- Data-room index.
- Transfer/adaptation scope defined in contract.

## Commercial qualification
Before quoting a final price, qualify: geography, intended operator, site/asset availability, financing capacity, desired exclusivity, adaptation scope and implementation horizon.
