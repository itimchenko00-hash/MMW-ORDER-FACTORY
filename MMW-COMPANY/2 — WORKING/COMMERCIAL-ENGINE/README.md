# MMW-COMPANY — COMMERCIAL ENGINE

## Purpose
Turn MMW-COMPANY from a project portfolio into a repeatable commercial system that generates paid leads, paid development work, project sales and partnership opportunities.

## Operating principle
**IDEA → BUSINESS MODEL → ECONOMICS → OPERATIONS → TECHNICAL → LEGAL → INVESTMENT → SALES → IMPLEMENTATION**

## Core offer
**MMW-COMPANY превращает бизнес-идею в готовый к реализации бизнес-проект.**

A READY-TO-SELL project means a developed and packaged business-project product. It does not mean that the business is already built, operating or guaranteed profitable.

## Revenue streams
1. Business development services.
2. READY-TO-SELL project sales.
3. Licensing / adaptation of project systems.
4. Partnership and equity participation where commercially justified.

## Protection rule
This commercial engine lives only in the Factory working layer. Existing protected/frozen repositories and locked project visuals are not modified by this package.
