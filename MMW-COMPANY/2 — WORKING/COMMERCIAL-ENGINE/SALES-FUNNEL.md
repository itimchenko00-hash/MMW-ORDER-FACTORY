# SALES FUNNEL

## Stage 1 — Attention
Channels: direct outreach, LinkedIn, Telegram, TikTok, YouTube, referrals, investor/entrepreneur communities, local business networks.

## Stage 2 — Lead
Capture: name, company, role, sector, geography, asset/site, project goal, budget range, timeline, source.

## Stage 3 — 20-minute qualification
Goal: determine whether there is a real business problem and whether MMW can create measurable project value.

## Stage 4 — Paid BUSINESS AUDIT
Deliverable: diagnosis, opportunity map, risk map, recommended next package.

## Stage 5 — Proposal
Choose: Business Model / Financial Model / Investment Package / Project Development / Project Adaptation.

## Stage 6 — Delivery
Milestones, acceptance criteria, payment schedule and decision log.

## Stage 7 — Expansion
Offer implementation support, adaptation, additional documentation, investor packaging or partnership.

## Stage 8 — Project sale / partnership
For suitable leads, present one of the six READY-TO-SELL projects or structure a project partnership.

## Core conversion targets for first 30 days
100 targeted prospects → 30 replies → 15 qualification calls → 5 paid audits → 2 larger projects.
These are operating targets, not forecasts or guarantees.
