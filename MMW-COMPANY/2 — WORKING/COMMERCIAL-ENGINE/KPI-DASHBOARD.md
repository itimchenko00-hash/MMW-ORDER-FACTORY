# KPI DASHBOARD

## Daily
- New targeted prospects
- First contacts sent
- Replies
- Follow-ups
- Qualification calls booked
- Paid audits sold
- Cash collected

## Weekly
- Leads by source
- Reply rate = replies / contacts
- Qualification rate = calls / replies
- Paid conversion = paid audits / qualification calls
- Proposal conversion = won deals / proposals
- Average deal value
- Pipeline value
- Cash collected

## Monthly
- Revenue by stream: services / project sales / licensing / partnerships
- Gross delivery load
- Outstanding receivables
- Best acquisition channel
- Best offer
- Best project
- Lost-deal reasons

## Minimum management rule
Track activity and cash separately. A large pipeline is not revenue. A READY-TO-SELL listing is not a sale until a contract and payment exist.
