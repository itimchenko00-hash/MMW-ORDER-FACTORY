## Common product definition
Each item is a business product developed by MMW-COMPANY. The buyer acquires the right to implement/adapt the project under the agreed commercial and legal structure.

## 100% package gate
Every product pack contains: product definition, buyer profile, deliverables, implementation phases, financial-model caveats and commercial form. The corresponding live presentation remains the visual sales surface; the pack is the operational handover specification.

## No false operating claims
Factory may present a project as READY-TO-SELL only. It must not represent an unimplemented project as an already operating company, completed facility, financed investment or proven financial result.

## Factory rule
Only active working layers are changed. FROZEN/BACKUPS/ARCHIVE layers remain untouched.
