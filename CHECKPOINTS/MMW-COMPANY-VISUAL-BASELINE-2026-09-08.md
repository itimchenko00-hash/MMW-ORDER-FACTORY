## Rule
The existing visual presentation of every project is frozen. Project packaging may add business/package data, documents, metadata and operational completeness, but must not replace, wrap, restyle, reorder, or visually reconstruct the established project pages.

## Commercial model
MMW-COMPANY develops ready-to-sell business projects. A project is not represented as already implemented unless implementation evidence exists.

Status language:
- `READY-TO-SELL BUSINESS PROJECT`
- `Developed by MMW-COMPANY`
- `Implementation: buyer / investor / operating partner`

`100% ready` means the business-project package is complete enough to sell and hand over; it does not mean guaranteed profitability or completed physical implementation.

## Factory rule
All mutable work stays in MMW-ORDER-FACTORY. Protected/conserved repositories remain untouched.
