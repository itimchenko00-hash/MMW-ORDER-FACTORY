# NEXUS WORK — MEDIA & PUBLIC PRESENTATION CONSTITUTION
Date: 2026-10-04

## 1. Project isolation
NEXUS WORK media belongs only to NEXUS WORK. Do not reuse CARPATHIA, ALADIN, AGROHUB, ENERGY PARK or generic MMW project photos when a dedicated NEXUS asset is required.

## 2. Replace, do not layer
When media is replaced, the previous mapping is removed before the new mapping is introduced. No duplicate image element, hidden legacy image, remote fallback or stacked media layer may remain.

## 3. One semantic block — one image
The hero and each of the eight NEXUS WORK expandable cards use a unique local image selected for the meaning of that block. The same file must not appear in two NEXUS blocks.

## 4. Local-first delivery
Public HTML must reference only local repository paths under /ASSETS/NEXUS-WORK/photos/. No external image URL may remain in the NEXUS page.

## 5. Source and rights register
Every imported photo must have a source page, author, license statement, source URL and semantic purpose recorded in PHOTO-SOURCES-2026-10-04.md.

## 6. Concept honesty
Photos are visual references only. They must not imply that the photographed building, location, tenant, price or business model is the actual NEXUS WORK project.

## 7. Interactivity
Opening a card must reveal its text and its corresponding image. Only the selected NEXUS card is open at a time.

## 8. Public cleanliness
No internal asset identifiers, source URLs, technical mapping syntax or development labels may be visible to visitors.

## 9. Final media QA
Before stabilization check: nine unique local files, nine semantic mappings, zero external image URLs, zero duplicated DOM image nodes, zero stale NEXUS media mappings, and successful browser loading of every mapped file.
